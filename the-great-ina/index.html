Extinct Ina: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Extinct Ina is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

We do not collect any data.

  • No account, no sign-in, no email, no name, no location, no contacts, no photos.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The game does not send anything over the internet. It works fully offline.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved games and settings (sound levels, camera, your dog's name) are stored only on your phone, inside the app. They never leave it. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Phone features the game uses

  • Keeps the screen on while you play (you can turn this off in Settings).
  • Vibration on hits (you can turn this off in Settings).

Children

The game has no chat, no purchases, no ads and collects nothing, from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app is.
