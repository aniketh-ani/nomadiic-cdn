Help us test Extinct Ina

A small pixel-art adventure about a lamp-lighter, her dog and one very long night. It is nearly ready, and we need real phones to show us what we missed. Two taps and you are in.

1Join the testers group

Use the Google account that is signed in to Play Store on your phone.

Join the group

Joining is instant. There is no chat, and no one in the group can see you.

2Become a tester on Google Play

Open this on your phone, tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play.

Open the test

If it says the app is not available, wait a minute and make sure it is the same Google account as step 1.

3Play, and keep it for two weeks

Please keep the game installed for 14 days and tell us what feels wrong. That is what lets a tiny studio release it properly.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads and no purchases.

What do I need?

An Android phone or tablet, Android 8 or newer, and a Google account.

What happens to my email?

Google shows it to the developer, only for running this test. Nobody else can see it, and it is not shared or sold.

Something broke. Where do I say?

Write to newaniketh@gmail.com, or use the feedback option in the Play Store listing.
