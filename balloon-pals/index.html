Ina and Balloons: Privacy Policy

Version 2. Last updated: 5 November 2026. This page covers the game Ina and Balloons (Android). It is written in English; if you would like it explained in another Indian language, write to us.

1. Who we are

Horse and Fog, an independent game studio in India ("we", "us"). Contact for every privacy question, request or complaint: newaniketh@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

2. The game collects no data

  • No account, no sign-in, no name, no email, no location, no contacts, no photos.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party code that collects data.
  • The app has no permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything from your phone.
  • Your progress, coins, in-game store items and settings are stored only on your phone, inside the app. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • On first launch the game stores one small note on your phone: that you accepted the terms, the version, and the date. It is not sent to us.
  • Phone feature used: vibration, for small taps of feedback. You can switch it off in Settings.

We do not sell or rent your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not build a profile of you. We may disclose it only if a court or authority legally requires us to. Our service provider (Google) may process it on servers outside India, under their own privacy policies.

Google Play handles your Google account and your use of Play under Google's own policies. As the developer, we can see crash and performance reports and counts that Google shares, such as crashes by phone model. They do not name you.

4. Your consent, and your rights

  • We use your information only with your consent, which you give freely and can refuse. If you do not consent, you simply cannot join the test or play.
  • You may at any time ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, ask for a copy, or name another person to act for you.
  • You may withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: email us, or in the game open Settings, then Withdraw consent. We then delete what we hold about you, except what the law makes us keep.
  • You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operating, or to the data protection authority where you live.

We follow the laws that apply to us, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules.

5. Children

The game and the test are for people aged 18 or over. A person under 18 may take part only if a parent or legal guardian has read this and the in-game terms and agreed for them, and gives the consent on their behalf. We do not track or monitor children's behaviour, show them ads, or target them in any way. If we learn that we hold a child's email without a parent or guardian's consent, we delete it.

6. Security

We keep tester emails only in our own email accounts and the Play Console list, and give access only to the people who run the test. No system is perfectly secure; if there is ever a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
